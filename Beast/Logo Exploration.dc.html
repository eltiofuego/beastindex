A claw mark that is also a rising rank

Every stroke is a tapered gash: broad blunt entry, sharp exit, one direction of bow, so the three read as a single motion rather than three parallel bars. The tips step upward left to right and each stroke reaches further than the last, which is what carries the chart reading. Gaps are deliberately uneven. B · the contained mark

It is the only construction that holds a solid silhouette at 16 pixels. The gash is negative space cut from a filled plate, so at small sizes the eye reads a strong block first and the three cuts second, which is the order the concept wants. It is one path with an even-odd fill: inside the plate the strokes subtract, past the edge they continue as solid marks, so the swipe genuinely breaks the container. Institutional in the plate, torn in the cut.
