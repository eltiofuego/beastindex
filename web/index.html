How your rank is calculated

Where the numbers come from

Every rank on this site is an empirical percentile — your number placed on the actual distribution of people in the pool you chose, for your sex and age band. Nothing is modelled or invented.

Strength is normalised with the DOTS coefficient, which removes bodyweight and sex bias. DOTS was chosen over Wilks on the evidence in Kopayev, Onyshchenko & Stetsenko (2020), which compared five powerlifting scoring formulas for fairness across weight classes.

One honest caveat: the competitive pools are made of people who entered a sanctioned meet or a big-city marathon. They are not the general public. If a number looks brutal, check which pool you are in — that is exactly why the control is there.
